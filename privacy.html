Privacy Policy

VYSH — Unisex Fashion Brand. Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Signing in

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with the shop so you can be signed in. We do not ask Google for anything else.

Orders and questions

Orders and questions you place or ask go to the shop owner only. The public shop never shows other customers’ orders or questions.

Your bag

Your shopping bag lives only in your browser session on your own device. It is not saved to an account and it does not follow you to another device.

Customise Studio

Text and logo images you upload in the Customise Studio are used only for that piece, in that visit. They are not sent or stored anywhere.

Checkout

Checkout on this site is in demo mode: no real payment is taken, and no card details are collected or stored.

Contact

If you have a question about your privacy, email us at vkrathor831@gmail.com.
