The VYSH idea

Designed once.
Worn by everyone.

VYSH began with a simple frustration: wardrobes split down the middle. So the brand keeps it to exactly four collections — Nail Art & Extensions for women, and Hoodies, T-Shirts and Sweatshirts cut unisex for men and women. Every clothing pattern is drafted on two bodies, graded in one run of sizes, and finished in colours that belong to no one — and everyone.

  • Cut for everybodyRelaxed blocks, adjustable waists and lengths that work across bodies.
  • Neutral by designInk, sand, stone and ivory — pieces that pair with everything you own.
  • Made to be keptHeavyweight fabrics and reinforced stitching, season after season.
